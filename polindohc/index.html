PolindoHC

Internal mobile app for PT Polindo Utama — lets employees browse upcoming company activities, register to attend, and track the ones they've joined.

The Client

PT Polindo Utama is an Indonesian recycling company. Their HC (Human Capital) team needed a way to announce and manage internal company activities — meetings, training sessions, and other events — without relying on manual communication.

What It Does

Employees open the app to see a feed of upcoming activities at the company. Each activity shows its name, description, location, date, and time. They can register to attend, and the ones they've joined appear in a dedicated "My Activities" view with a countdown to the next event. Online events show a direct join link.

Features

  • Activity feed with upcoming company events
  • Register or join activities in a few taps
  • My Activities view with countdown timer and online join links
  • Push notification reminders before events start
  • Admin: manage employees and toggle admin access
  • Admin: create and manage the activity list (online/offline)

Tech Stack

  • React Native
  • Google Firebase

Access

Internal app for PT Polindo Utama employees. Not publicly available.
