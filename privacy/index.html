Privacy

Updated 8 November 2026

matsuokengo uploader

matsuokengo uploader is a command-line tool I use to upload and manage videos on my own YouTube channel. It asks Google for access to YouTube on the account that signs in.

The tool runs only on my own computer. It keeps the sign-in token in that computer's keychain and sends data only to Google's YouTube API. It has no server of its own and shares nothing with anyone.

To remove its access, open Google account permissions and remove matsuokengo uploader.

Each app I publish, such as Undirect, has its own privacy policy on its own site.
